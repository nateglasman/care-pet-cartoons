Rules (as implemented)

  
    
    
  • A day counts when all four tasks are done for that pet on the same local calendar day.
    • 
    
  • Tiers: Gold (0) → Shiny Gold (1+) → Platinum (10+) → Holo (30+) → Legendary (50+). XP multipliers 1 / 1.1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 2.
    • 
    
  • Task XP: feed 10, bath 15, fetch 15 (+1 per metre of best throw), walk 20 (+1 per 100 m). Full day +50. Milestones at 10 / 30 / 50 days: +100 / +300 / +500. All × tier multiplier.
    • 
    
  • Every 7 straight days banks a streak freeze (max 2) that covers one missed day automatically.
